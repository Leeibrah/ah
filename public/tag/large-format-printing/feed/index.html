Exhibition Trailers

Unique exhibition trailers featuring state of the art mechanics and AV equipment – then they are layered with Sign & Prints’ final graphics touch…

Our introduction to Vipex came in 2008 with our first successful tender for their requirements for a vehicle colour change, the brief was to totally wrap a truck trailer unit called “Big TV”, a 14m long and 3.5m high black trailer unit was to be wrapped in white vinyl and dressed in Kia Motors Livery for the 2008 UEFA European Championships in Austria with a 3 day turnaround and only a few days notice Sign & Print met Vipex’s expectations and were satisfied with our capabilities to go on and instruct us with their wrapping and graphics requirements ever since.

Our relationship has continued to grow to where Vipex fully entrust us to liaise directly with their clients regarding the marketing and livery needs for their mechanical wonders, we have to date dressed in excess of 40 units for Vipex and look forward to creating more visual spectacles with them going forward.
